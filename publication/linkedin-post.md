# LinkedIn post

I’m sharing my latest Power BI portfolio project: **KMB Manufacturing — Quality & Operations Analytics**.

I designed this four-page solution around a fictional ISO 9001:2015 manufacturing scenario to demonstrate how one governed semantic model can support different levels of decision-making:

- Executive quality and operations oversight
- Line-level production control
- Tactical KPI and corrective-action management
- Advanced quality investigation and Pareto analysis

The project includes a portable PBIP structure, a star-schema semantic model, governed KPI targets, DAX measures, Power Query transformations, dynamic weekly and monthly reporting, accessible status design, and fully synthetic reproducible data.

The complete source, documentation, validation controls, and setup instructions are available in the GitHub repository:

**Repository:** https://github.com/Sigma-KBM/KMB-Manufacturing-Dashboard

This is a fictional portfolio demonstration and does not represent a real company or an official certification.
