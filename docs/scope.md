# Scope and Success Criteria

## Business context

KMB Manufacturing is a fictional ISO 9001:2015-certified manufacturer of modular industrial water-filtration assemblies. The reporting solution models production, quality inspection, MRB, corrective action, labor, and training outcomes for a 24-month closed synthetic history.

## Audiences and decisions

| Report | Audience | Decisions supported |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership Quality & Operations | Plant, Quality, and Operations directors | Select enterprise improvement priorities; direct investment; assess quality, capacity, and workforce risk. |
| Line Operations Control | Line managers, supervisors, and teams | Contain issues; prioritize shift actions; manage production, quality, MRB, and readiness. |
| Tactical Performance Management | Department heads | Track 13-week and monthly target attainment; assign recovery actions; adjust weekly/monthly strategy. |
| Advanced Quality Analytics | Analysts and SMEs | Investigate trends, patterns, outliers, defect concentration, and hypotheses. |

## Non-goals

- This demonstration does not establish real ISO compliance, product certification, causal root cause, employee performance management, or production deployment readiness.
- Analytical association visuals do not establish causation.
- No real data may be introduced without explicit authorization and a separate privacy/security assessment.

## Success criteria

1. All published measures reconcile to independently calculated source controls.
2. Targets come solely from `Fact_KpiTargets`.
3. Each report opens and refreshes in Power BI Desktop after a close-and-reopen cycle.
4. Every main visual has meaningful English alt text, logical tab order, adequate contrast, and a non-color status cue.
5. A clean copy can be opened and refreshed following only the README.
